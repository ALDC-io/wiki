### Architecture

- [[agent-action-safety-gate]] — how an ALDC agent expresses its safety policy ONCE across every action of every vertical (the [[triage-agent]] generalization-platform core, R19-22): the router emits a typed **`ActionEnvelope`** (action_type · risk_class · mode=propose/execute · evidence refs · handler_id); a **handler manifest** declares each action's risk_class (verticals as peers, gate reads danger from data not a hardcode); a uniform **`risk_class × mode` guard matrix** yields the disposition (read→auto · internal_write→auto-draft/gated · external_write→approval · privileged→approval/dual), fail-closed. **Crux: the gate is a fail-closed assertion layer ABOVE the proven enforcer (`safety.py`), not a rewrite — byte-identical enforcement, can only prevent never weaken.** Includes the discipline for proving a safety-critical refactor changed nothing (unchanged suite + exhaustive no-auto-execute invariant + a LIVE shadow-diff + a dormant trip-wire). Sits around the pipeline [[agent-observability-telemetry]] instruments.
