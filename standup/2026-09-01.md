## Why the Fusion92 bugs got complicated

Two reported symptoms became **three defects**, and the hardest one isn't a code fix.

- **FU92-429 — the client's edit was correct; the warehouse silently undoes it.** When all three DAX
  platform IDs go NULL, the flight's **Smartsheet** IDs get substituted. Proven byte-identical
  across three layers. **677 flights** affected, zero unexplained.
- **So 429 needs a product decision, not a change** — those 677 flights depend on the current
  behaviour. What *should* a cleared flight show?
- **FU92-428 — fix shipped, diagnosis still unproven.** Export now POSTs the flight IDs instead of a
  4,833-char query string. But the failing request was never captured, and two *larger* jobs (207
  and 177 flights) are unreported — if one exports fine, the hypothesis dies.
- **FU92-430 — found on the way.** A 10h30m nightly sync blackout (23:50–10:20 UTC). We'd published
  "every 30 minutes"; that holds only 14 hours a day. The stale figure had misled a client and
  mis-framed the first half of the 428 investigation.
