# FU92-421 — DAX flight actuals stop early (Windsor account drop + Meta campaign rebuild)

Reported by Juliann Otto 2026-08-07: three flights showing spend that stops before the flight
ends. Resolved by `/inquest` (5-member adversarial council + synthesiser re-verification).

**Status: QA.** Cause B fixed and verified. Cause A blocked on client action.

## The two causes — they looked identical and were unrelated

| | Cause A | Cause B |
|---|---|---|
| Flights | E8TWD, RFT7V, **7IHPM** (unreported) | GMX2U |
| Cause | 3 LinkedIn ad accounts dropped out of [[Windsor]] on 2026-07-21 | Meta campaign rebuilt; flight never updated |

Accounts `509879445` (ADM → E8TWD), `502845846` (ADM Investor Services → RFT7V + 7IHPM),
`506641008` (BCBSM, latent) all stopped being ingested at **one shared load instant,
`2026-07-21 10:16:25.681-07:00`**, while `500938269` (Nationwide Pet) kept loading daily.
`508272220` (U-M Ross) had dropped the same way on 2026-05-31.

Proven Windsor-side: a direct call to the Windsor LinkedIn connector with the Fusion92 key returns
**only `500938269`** — the others are absent even for pre-cutoff dates Snowflake already holds.
Full detection runbook now lives in [[Windsor]] § *silent account-drop failure mode*.

Same failure mode as [[FU92-415]] (Smile Doctors / Meta). **Fusion92's LinkedIn feed is down to
1 live account of 5** — progressive decay, not a one-off.

⚠ **On reconnection, verify the backfill reaches back to 2026-07-22.** A recent-window-only pull
leaves the gap permanently missing.

## Cause B — GMX2U: campaign rebuilt for a goal change (FIXED)

The client asked ~2026-05-28 to switch the Meta optimisation goal to Form Submissions. Meta cannot
change that on a live campaign, so it was rebuilt 06-01 as `Q22026_RTR_Pilot_GMX2U_FormSubmits`;
the original drained by 06-07. The flight still named the retired campaign, so **$26,526 of live
spend belonged to no flight at all**.

Identity established on six independent signals — all three ad set names reused 1:1, clean one-day
handover, the flight's own `PRIMARY_KPI` already reading "Form Submissions", campaign name carrying
the flight code, budget fit, and exclusivity within PRJ002347.

Applied 2026-08-07 by **appending** (not replacing) campaign `120246436851970753` + its 3 ad sets to
the flight doc via `application/update`. Consumer layer (`DAX_API.DAILY_FLIGHT_METRICS_NO_DAX`):
**$281.06 → $27,462.41**, range 06-01 → 08-09, three monthly rows instead of one.

## ⭐ Durable lessons

1. **Aggregate at the wrong grain and you will refute the right hypothesis.** "Connector outage" was
   dismissed because `PLATFORM_ID = 'Direct-LinkedIn'` showed `MAX(SPEND_DATE)` = today — carried
   entirely by the one surviving account. **Freshness must be grouped by ad ACCOUNT, never by
   platform.** Four council members independently re-derived this after inheriting the bad frame.
2. **The best-fitting documented failure mode was the wrong one.** [[FU92-419]] (blank platform IDs
   from the sync window) fit the story perfectly and was refuted in minutes: these flights had
   complete IDs, and a blank-ID flight shows total `$0`, not partial spend.
3. **An absence of rows is not an absence of spend — prove your instrument can see zero.** This feed
   *does* land literal `$0.00` rows when a campaign is live but not delivering (450 on `509879445`).
   That negative control is what turned "maybe the campaigns ended" into "the data was never loaded".
4. **A partial final day is a load artifact, not a taper.** Final-day spend at 55–61% of run-rate
   across two unrelated accounts = one load that ran mid-day, not two campaigns winding down.
5. **`application/update` is a PARTIAL MERGE and takes changed fields under a `document` key**
   (`workflows/.../workflows/netsuite.py:541`). Sending a full flat document returns **HTTP 200 and
   silently writes nothing** — a 200 here is not proof of a write. Always re-read and diff.
6. **Two identical-looking symptoms can have unrelated causes.** One report, one date pattern, two
   root causes in different layers with different owners. Bracketing by account was what split them.
7. **Calibrate an alert against real data before shipping it.** The freshness monitor's first run
   produced 21 findings, mostly long-retired accounts — true and useless. Capping the alert window
   at 21 days dark cut it to 5 actionable. An un-calibrated alert gets muted, which is how the
   original failure survived 17 days.

## ⚠ Do NOT loosen the LinkedIn `ORDER_ID` join

The tempting fix (LEFT JOIN / drop the predicate) admits **+$119,922 (+16.9%)** of correctly-excluded
LinkedIn spend across **23,883** (account, campaign_group) pairs claimed by more than one flight —
worst by 97 — which `FCT_DAILY_SPEND`'s even-split logic smears across unrelated flights. And it
fixes nothing: zero raw rows exist to admit. Same shape as the [[GP-311]] trap. Widening dates,
`TRIM`-ing tokens, forcing a dynamic-table refresh and re-running the FU92-419 reconcile are all
provable no-ops here.

## Prevention

Account-freshness monitor built for the [[observability-platform]] (Tier 1 account-dark /
Tier 2 feed-dark, so a *total* outage isn't masked by the absence of a healthy sibling). Runs on the
existing obs-jobs runner — no new infrastructure, no new credentials, ~$0 marginal Snowflake cost.
**Built and validated, not yet deployed.** Realises [[FU92-379]].

It immediately surfaced three things nobody had reported: **Smile Doctors dark 92 days**
([[FU92-415]], "Ready for Customer" since 06-11 — never recovered), Meta
`HD - Talent Acquisition` (6d), Google `N|H - Lincoln Park` (9d).

The durable fix is organisational: a dedicated Fusion92 integration user with standing agency access,
so the feed does not depend on one person's ad-account permissions decaying.
