# GP-322 — Navira allocated Amazon ad cost reads 3.6× campaign spend in the current month

**Bug · blocks [[GP-317]] · assigned Paul · label `navira-roadmap` · status Consulting/Design**
**Verdict: ROOT CAUSE PROVEN. Fix identified and validated in a sandbox. NOT DEPLOYED.**

Navira's Daily/Sales model (`Data Model 66151728`) carries two Amazon ad figures that are the same money
presented two ways — campaign spend, and that spend re-allocated onto order lines. They agree within ~2%
in every closed month and **inverted in the current one: $163,689 allocated against $45,198 spend (+262%)**.
Resolved by `/inquest` 2026-08-10 (5 blind council members). Full evidence:
`aldc-launchpad/docs/evidence/gp322.md` @ commit `8def4e6`, branch `feature/navira-ad-metrics-request`.

## Root cause

**A circular dependency broken by materialisation order** — the general pattern is written up separately
at [[circular-dependency-build-order]], which is the page to read for the reusable lesson.

`WAREHOUSE_SOURCE.SALES_DIM_ORDER` appends rows read back out of `WAREHOUSE.SALES_FCT_COST`
(`ATTRIBUTION_TYPE = 'No Match'` — the `UNK_` placeholders of
[[gep-unknown-order-placeholders]]), while the cost fact's own allocation **denominator** reads that
dimension. Snowflake breaks the cycle by build order:

| time | object | role |
|---|---|---|
| 06:00:01 | `SALES_DIM_ORDER_BASE` | the allocation's **consumer** side — T-0 fresh |
| **06:02:32** | **`SALES_FCT_COST`** | the allocation materialises here |
| **06:02:48** | **`SALES_DIM_ORDER`** | the allocation's **denominator** — **T-1 stale** |

So the share's denominator saw yesterday's order list and its numerator saw today's. On 2026-08-09 the
denominator held **5.1%** of the sales it should have (6,744 of 132,439; 3,419 lines / 3,328 orders
missing). Structural since the view deployed **2026-03-24**; fires on every build.
**The allocation arithmetic itself is correct.**

## ⚠ Scope: four months, not August — and margin moves UP

Three frozen copies of `SALES_FCT_ORDERLINE` (left by unrelated rollback work) **photograph what was
actually published**:

| Read on | MTD as published | Correct | Overstated |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2026-06-19 | 181,582 | 103,600 | **+75.3%** |
| 2026-06-25 | 212,742 | 144,445 | **+47.3%** |
| 2026-07-08 | 154,709 | 39,184 | **+294.8%** |
| 2026-08-10 | 163,689 | 45,191 | **+262.2%** |

Worst *relative* error **early in a month** — exactly when a client compares this-month vs last-month.

**Closed months are correct and always have been:** 18 divergent days of **1,318**, nothing older than
21 days, 98.7% of the error in the newest 3 days, **$0.00 beyond age 22 across 1,297 days**.

⚠ **The error UNDERSTATES margin, so correcting it raises the client's numbers.** August
`Actual - Margin - Net` reads **122,718** against a corrected **241,209** (−49.1%); margin rate
**10.06% vs 19.77%** (−9.71pp). Against Jan–Jul at 18–21%, August reads as a margin collapse. It is not.

## The fix — validated in sandbox, not deployed

Repoint **two** denominator joins (`ASIN_SALES` L64, `BRAND_SALES` L134) from `WAREHOUSE.SALES_DIM_ORDER`
to `WAREHOUSE.SALES_DIM_ORDER_BASE` — the object the consumer side already reads. This removes the cycle,
so no build-order change is needed.

Shadow-materialised into `WAREHOUSE_TEST_PAUL` and measured:
- conserves on **every** day including the newest — ratios **0.992–1.007** vs campaign spend
- **42 of 44 months change by exactly $0.00** (full history to 2023-01); only 2026-07 (−65.68, −0.039%)
  and August move
- **schema parity exact** (14 columns, same order) — a drop-in
- **zero** references to the stale dimension remain, so it is immune by construction, not by scheduling
- rollback + apply scripts committed, **unexecuted**, diff verified to contain only those two lines

Alternatives rejected with numbers: normalising the share restates signed-off history (+1,120.54);
dropping the newest N days deletes 31.8% of August's real spend at N=3; **reordering the build cannot be
done literally** (the dim reads the fact) and is not provably a fixed point.

⭐ **Nothing renders these measures.** Enumerated across all 27 workspaces: the client's live dataset has
2 reports and neither uses them. The exposure route is the **field list** — their own pivot /
Analyze-in-Excel. So the [[GP-293]]-style *"every visual paints"* gate **cannot be run here**; the
consumer-layer gate has to be a **DAX/pivot parity check** of the calendar-year YTD gesture.

**Also open:** PROD cadence is `NOT-VISIBLE` (inbound share, `GET_DDL` blocked — though PROD's *rendered*
figures are clean because its model refreshes **hourly** and never sits at the skewed instant); and a
**second cycle edge** is unmeasured — `WAREHOUSE_SOURCE.SALES_FCT_SELLER_CLOUD_ORDERLINE` reads
`WAREHOUSE.SALES_FCT_COST_HISTORY` and builds at 06:01:48, *before* the cost fact.

## Method notes worth keeping

- **H2 (PBI incremental-refresh partition boundary), recorded on the ticket as the "strongest lead", was
  dead in one query**: the warehouse-vs-DAX gap is **$0.00**, so PBI is innocent. Running that test first
  halved the search space, exactly as the ticket predicted it would.
- **The mechanism was found by table-vs-live-view**, a test needing no theory, after three theory-driven
  reconstructions failed. See [[circular-dependency-build-order]].
- **Seven hypotheses were refuted, four of them the orchestrator's own**, including an age gradient
  misread as a calendar onset date (withdrawn independently by two investigators) and a settling-curve
  model that proved *confounded* rather than imprecise.
- Client correction document rewritten and **still held**:
  `aldc-launchpad/docs/evidence/gp317/navira-ad-cost-correction.html`.
