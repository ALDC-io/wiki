## Multi-tenant model (the reusable pattern)
- **Tenant key = `ENTITY_CODE`** on the unified marketing fact + every `MARKETING_EFFICIENCY` view; **`SHARED_DIM_ENTITY`** maps platform accounts → entity (the marketing-side isolation gate; INNER JOIN prevents foreign-account leakage).
- **Onboard a tenant = new `ENTITY_CODE` rows + a `SHARED_DIM_ENTITY` entry** — no new tables, no measure changes. Validated end-to-end with LECTRIC.
- **Agency = sales-only until ad access:** with no spend, MER is undefined; the tenant shows actual revenue. Tier-3 product-grounding needs a **per-entity product dim** (agency ASINs aren't in Navira's Sellercloud master). See [[cross-channel-marketing-attribution]].

## ⚠️ Lectric Contribution Margin = data gap (2026-06-15)
Lectric shows in the Contribution-Margin layer's marketing side (665 `MARKETING_EFFICIENCY` rows) but at **0% COGS coverage** — and this is a **data gap, not a view bug**. `SALES_FCT_LECTRIC_AMAZON_ORDERLINE` (4,464 rows, stable BASE TABLE) carries sales (`SALES_GROSS_QUANTITY/TRANSACTION/TAX`) but **zero cost columns** — no `SALES_COGS_*`, no margin. Lectric's 25 products are Amazon ASINs (`B0BVNJYMLN`…); **0 of 25 appear in the SellerCloud-keyed cost-history**, and there is no Lectric cost source anywhere in TEST. So Lectric contribution margin cannot be computed until Lectric **per-ASIN COGS is acquired + landed** (Lectric is Amazon-direct, no SellerCloud SITECOST/LANDEDCOST). Adding a LECTRIC branch to the margin view's `COGS_DAY` CTE is necessary but insufficient (nothing to sum). The PBI flags spec shows Lectric margin as **N/A**, not zero, until then. Diag: `aldc-launchpad/warehouse_ops/_lectric_cm_diagnose.py`. See [[cross-channel-marketing-attribution]] (Contribution Margin), [[GP-PENDING-missing-cogs-cost-history]].
